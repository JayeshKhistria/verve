About Verve

Cleaning built on trust, care, and consistency.

Our Story

A local team focused on doing the basics exceptionally well

Verve Cleaning began with a straightforward goal: provide businesses with a cleaning company they can rely on. That means arriving as scheduled, following the agreed scope, communicating clearly, and treating every property with care.

Today, we support offices, strata buildings, clinics, retail locations, warehouses, and other commercial properties throughout Metro Vancouver.
